## SEO techniques carried into Matcha

- **JK6 main:** natural Chinese brand and service intent in titles, opening copy and useful sections. Matcha targets Malaysian companionship, not casino intent. No unrelated brand names or metadata are copied into deployed files.
- **Check schema and LLM crawling:** a central origin and shared Organization/WebSite/WebPage IDs; inline JSON-LD available in initial HTML; per-page exported JSON-LD; directory URLs derived from the same route list as the sitemap; bot-identity HTTP checks; schema and metadata validation.
- **JK6 SEO recovery checkpoints:** measure actual Search Console outcomes after release. Branded query gains from another project are not a promise for Matcha. Discovery, submitted sitemaps and indexing are separate signals.
- **Eventflo main:** avoid inconsistent apex/www and canonical signals. Final deployment needs a verified Matcha domain, one preferred origin, and a matching redirect/DNS configuration. HTTP 200 is not proof Google processed a sitemap or indexed a page.

Google's current guidance says ordinary SEO foundations also support AI search. `llms.txt` is optional and does not improve Google ranking or guarantee AI citations. FAQ content is useful for visitors, but no FAQ rich-result promise is made. The prototype uses factual entity schema without made-up ratings or prices.
